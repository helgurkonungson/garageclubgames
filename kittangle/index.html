LOGIC PUZZLE / AGES 13+

Kittangle

Every arrow is a long, stretchy cat. Tap one and it slides off the board, if the way is clear. Bonk another cat and you lose a heart.

> PLAY IN BROWSER GOOGLE PLAY / SOON CRAZYGAMES / SOON

HOW TO PLAY

  1. 1

    Tap a cat

    It slides out in the direction its head is facing.

  2. 2

    Clear the path

    If another cat is in the way, yours bonks it, bounces back and you lose a heart.

  3. 3

    Free the board

    You get 3 hearts per level. Free every cat to earn stars and move on.

WHY YOU WILL LIKE IT

  • 300 levels that start gentle and get clever, from small 5x5 boards to big ones.
  • A cozy cafe to grow with the stars you earn.
  • A fresh daily puzzle with streaks, and weekly events.
  • Boosters when you are stuck: Hint, Feed and an extra Heart.
  • Available in 10 languages. No account needed.
  • Free to play. Rewarded ads are always optional, and a one-time No Ads purchase removes the rest.
