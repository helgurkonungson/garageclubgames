Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

The short version. Kittangle has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or exact location. Your progress is stored on your device. Ads, purchases and game platforms involve some technical identifiers, explained below.

1. Who we are

Kittangle is published by GarageClub Games, operated by Oleg Tsarkov, an individual entrepreneur registered in the Republic of Armenia (registration no. 286.1553696, TIN 20064196), 12 V. Vagharshyan St., Arabkir, Yerevan 0012, Armenia. We are the data controller for the processing described in this policy. Contact: support@garageclubgames.ru.

2. What data is involved

Game data on your device

Progress, settings, language, collected items and purchase status are saved on your device, in the app or in your browser storage. On some web portals the portal may also keep a cloud save under your portal account. We do not receive this data on servers of our own.

Anonymous gameplay events

We may record events such as "level started", "level completed", "booster used", "ad shown" and "purchase completed", together with basic technical details like app version, platform, language and country. We use them to balance difficulty and to find problems. These events are not linked to your name, email address or any account.

Advertising data

Kittangle shows ads. On Android they are served by Google AdMob. On web portals they are served by the portal's own advertising systems. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, these partners may process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and device and app information. Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, we ask for your consent first. See Ads and consent.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play or by the portal where you play. We receive confirmation that a purchase happened (product, status and a purchase token) but never your payment card details.

Messages you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use it to answer you and to improve the game.

Technical data from stores and portals

Google Play, game portals and similar services may give us aggregated statistics and crash or error reports, for example installs by country or crashes by device model. These do not identify you.

3. Why we process data

If the GDPR or the UK GDPR applies to you, we rely on these legal bases:

  • Contract: to provide the game, restore purchases and run features you use.
  • Consent: for personalized advertising and for access to advertising identifiers where consent is required. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: for anonymous statistics, fixing bugs, keeping the game secure, preventing fraud and answering your messages.
  • Legal obligation: where we must keep records, for example for tax and accounting.

4. Who receives data

  • Google (AdMob and Google Play). See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data when you use partner apps.
  • Game portals and platforms where Kittangle is published, for example CrazyGames or Yandex Games. Each acts under its own privacy policy.
  • Service providers that host our website and receive our email, such as Cloudflare.

We do not sell your personal data. We may disclose data to authorities where the law obliges us to.

5. International transfers

We are based in Armenia. Our partners, including Google, process data in other countries such as the United States. Where required, they use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

6. How long we keep data

  • Game data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the game.
  • Anonymous events and statistics are kept only as long as they are useful for improving the game.
  • Emails are kept as long as needed to answer you and for a reasonable time afterwards.
  • Advertising partners keep data under their own retention rules.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because Kittangle has no accounts, we usually cannot link data to you unless you give us a technical identifier. To delete local data or reset ad identifiers, see how to delete your data. To make a request, email support@garageclubgames.ru. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

Kittangle is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We keep very little data and work with reputable partners. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. For significant changes we will say so on this page.
