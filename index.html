ABOUT THE STUDIO

GarageClub Games is an independent studio from Yerevan, Armenia. We make light, friendly games and apps that are easy to start and hard to put down.

Everything is designed, built and supported by one person, Oleg Tsarkov, with a lot of help from AI tools. Players come first: clear rules, fair ads, and no accounts unless a game truly needs them.
