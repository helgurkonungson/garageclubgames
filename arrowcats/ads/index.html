Ads and consent

Last updated: 15 November 2026

ArrowCats is free to play and ads help us keep it that way. We try to keep ads fair: the ones that give you something are always your choice.

How ads work

  • Rewarded ads are always optional. Watch one to earn a hint, a heart or Fish. Nothing is forced.
  • Interstitial ads may appear between levels. We keep them rare: never in the first levels, never back to back and never right after a rewarded ad.
  • Banners may appear in menus on some platforms.
  • A one-time No Ads purchase removes the ads that appear without your choice.

Who serves the ads

On Android, ads are served by Google AdMob. On web portals, ads are served by the portal's own advertising systems. These partners may use device identifiers to show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Your consent choices

In the EEA, the UK and other places where consent is required, ArrowCats asks for your choice with Google's consent tool before it loads ads that need consent. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized. You can review or change your choice at any time from the privacy options in the game's settings. On web portals, consent is handled by the portal.

Other ways to limit ad tracking

  • On Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in the device settings.
  • On the web, use Google Ads Settings.
