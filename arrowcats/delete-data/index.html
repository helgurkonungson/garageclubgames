How to delete your data

Last updated: 15 November 2026

ArrowCats has no accounts, so there is no account to close. Your progress lives on your device and you can erase it yourself in a minute.

Android app

  1. Open Settings, then Apps, then ArrowCats.
  2. Tap Storage, then Clear data (or Clear storage). You can also uninstall the app.

This erases your progress and settings on that device. Purchases stay on your Google account and can be restored. Menu names vary a little between devices.

In a browser

Clear the site data of the page where you play. In Chrome, open Settings, then Privacy and security, then Site settings, then "View permissions and data stored across sites", search for the site and delete its data. Other browsers have a similar option under cookies and site data.

Some portals keep a cloud save under your portal account. Manage or delete that in the portal's account settings.

Advertising identifiers

  • Android: in Settings, find the Google ads settings (the path differs by device) and choose "Delete advertising ID".
  • Web: Google Ads Settings lets you turn off personalized ads.
  • EEA and UK: you can change your consent choice, see Ads and consent.

Ask us to delete data

Email support@garageclubgames.ru with the subject "Data deletion". Tell us which platform you play on. If you have it, include your advertising ID or a purchase order number so we can look for related records. We will reply within 30 days.

Because we hold no account data, in most cases there is nothing beyond anonymous statistics that we cannot link to you. If you wrote to us by email, we will delete that correspondence on request unless the law requires us to keep it.
